Formwork and scaffolding play a crucial role in construction projects, providing support for concrete structures during pouring and curing. Brands like [Doka](https://www.doka.com/en/index), [PERI](https://www.peri.com/en/competences/formwork-systems.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com), and Titan offer modular systems that streamline the process.

In this guide, we’ll walk through the step-by-step configuration method for designing formwork and scaffolding components without having to model each element from scratch.

###### Step 1: Understand the Project Requirements

Before selecting a formwork system, you need to analyze:

- The structure’s geometry (walls, columns, slabs, beams, soffits, etc.)
- Load-bearing capacity
- Pouring sequence and construction schedule
- Site conditions and access constraints
- Safety requirements

Using this information, you can determine the most suitable formwork system.

*For a comprehensive overview of formwork, check out [Formwork - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formwork).*

###### Step 2: Choose the Right Formwork System

Manufacturers like Doka and PERI provide a variety of modular formwork solutions. Some common options include:

- [Wall Formwork](https://direct.doka.com/_ext/downloads/downloadcenter/999729002_2022_01_online.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Doka Framax, PERI TRIO
- [Slab Formwork](https://direct.doka.com/_ext/downloads/downloadcenter/999776002_2024_08_online.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Doka Dokadek 30, PERI SKYDECK
- [Beam and Column Formwork](https://www.formtechinc.com/documents/forming-systems/clamp-systems/doka-framax/Doka-Framax-User-Information-Guide.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Doka Top 50, PERI VARIO

Consult the manufacturer’s technical catalog to match the system to your project requirements.

![formwork-design-component](https://formworkforconcrete.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/formwork-design-component.jpeg)

###### Step 3: Configure the Formwork Using Manufacturer Guidelines

Most formwork suppliers provide detailed configuration manuals and software tools to assist with setup. The process generally involves:

1. **Defining panel layouts**: Based on structural dimensions and joint alignment.
2. **Determining connection points**: Using clamps, bolts, or locks.
3. **Adding bracing and supports**: Ensuring lateral stability.
4. **Verifying tie rod placement**: Preventing bulging due to concrete pressure.
5. **Allowing for access points**: For safe worker movement and concrete pouring.

###### Step 4: Integrate Scaffolding for Support and Access

Scaffolding is often required for:

- Worker access to high areas
- Bracing formwork against lateral forces
- Supporting loads during concrete placement

Common systems include:

- **PERI UP Scaffolding**: Flexible modular design
- **Doka Load-Bearing Towers**: Adjustable for different heights
- **Cuplock and Ringlock Systems**: Ideal for complex structures

Ensure compliance with safety regulations and load calculations before installation.

###### Step 5: Conduct a Pre-Pour Inspection

![Monitor During and After Concrete Pouring](https://formworkforconcrete.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/pouring-concrete.jpeg)

Before pouring concrete, conduct a thorough inspection:

- **Check for alignment and levelness**
- **Ensure all fasteners and supports are secure**
- **Confirm proper reinforcement placement**
- **Verify accessibility for pouring and finishing**
- **Inspect safety measures (guardrails, ladders, fall protection, etc.)**

###### Conclusion

Designing formwork and scaffolding efficiently is crucial for safe and cost-effective construction.

By following manufacturer guidelines and leveraging modular systems, you can configure components quickly and ensure structural integrity.

For those interested in learning more, manufacturer-provided training or software like Doka Tipos and PERI CAD can further streamline the process.
