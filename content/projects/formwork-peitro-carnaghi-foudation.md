excerpt: "Partial Removal of Existing Foundation and Stelcore Piles\nThe project involves the partial removal of the existing foundation. Stelcore piles from the current structure will be removed below the bottom level of the newly proposed foundation block. The machine foundation will be constructed on the demolished portion of the old foundation, ensuring a stable base for the new installation while accommodating the necessary structural adjustments. Careful coordination will be required to ensure proper alignment and support for the new foundation"
