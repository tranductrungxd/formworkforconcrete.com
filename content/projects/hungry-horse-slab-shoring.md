## From drawing to site

Two sheets were enough for this job: a lumber plan and a props plan for the slab and beam, then sections, the beam detail and a 3D view of the typical slab formwork. The photos from site show the system standing as drawn: steel props under timber beams, with the beam formwork carried on its own props.

![3D view of the slab formwork: lumber joists on doubled lumber beams over steel props](https://res.cloudinary.com/oceanbim/image/upload/v1791461909/formworkforconcrete.com/projects/hungry-horse-slab-shoring/hh-3d-slab-system.png)

![Site photo: steel props and timber beams supporting the slab and beam formwork](https://res.cloudinary.com/oceanbim/image/upload/v1791461912/formworkforconcrete.com/projects/hungry-horse-slab-shoring/hh-site-shoring-wide.jpg)

## What we delivered

- A slab and beam lumber plan: the first layer of 2×6 lumber and the second layer of doubled 2×6 beams, laid out separately for the slab and the beam
- A props support plan locating every slab prop and beam prop on the grid
- Sections through the slab and beam, with the U-head formwork jacks, the bracing and the wall formwork at the existing walls
- A detail of the beam bottom and a 3D view of the typical slab formwork system

![Slab and beam lumber plan next to the props support plan](https://res.cloudinary.com/oceanbim/image/upload/v1791461911/formworkforconcrete.com/projects/hungry-horse-slab-shoring/hh-lumber-and-props-plan.png)

![Sections through the slab and beam formwork showing props, bracing and the beam detail](https://res.cloudinary.com/oceanbim/image/upload/v1791461910/formworkforconcrete.com/projects/hungry-horse-slab-shoring/hh-sections-sheet.png)

## Formwork system

Plywood decking on lumber, two layers of 2×6 lumber, and adjustable steel props with U-heads. The beam has its own row of props and a lumber bottom form, and the props are braced.

![Site photo: steel props under the beam and slab formwork](https://res.cloudinary.com/oceanbim/image/upload/v1791461913/formworkforconcrete.com/projects/hungry-horse-slab-shoring/hh-site-shoring-props.jpg)
